# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Members, contributors, and leaders pledge to keep participation free from harassment. This applies to everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, and education. It also applies regardless of socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

* Show empathy and kindness.
* Respect different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
* Give and accept constructive feedback.
* Take responsibility, apologize to people affected by mistakes, and learn from them.
* Focus on what helps the whole community, not only individuals.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
* Other conduct that could reasonably seem inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards. They take fair, appropriate corrective action for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the community in public. Examples include using an official e-mail address or posting through an official social media account. They also include serving as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement privately through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** (GitHub's private reporting channel). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders must respect the reporter's privacy and security.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders use these Community Impact Guidelines to choose consequences for violations of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**: Community leaders give a private, written warning. It explains the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. They may request a public apology.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through 1 incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning includes consequences for continued behavior. It bans interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with Code of Conduct enforcers, for a set time. This includes community spaces and external channels such as social media. A violation may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban stops all interaction or public communication with the community for a set time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with Code of Conduct enforcers, is allowed during this time. A violation may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: A pattern of violating community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior or harassment of an individual. It also includes aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from all public interaction within the community.
